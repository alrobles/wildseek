# WildSeek — 3-minute demo script

On-screen footer throughout: "Ecological surveillance for wildlife
professionals — not medical advice." Badge "built during the event"
fixed under the video for its whole duration.

## Cold open — required declaration (0:00–0:12)
- Screen: single slate, two columns: "pre-existing" (xnicher,
  oneearthr, the PNAS 2022 three-distance framework, phyloseek
  mammal tree, BirdTree/VertLife, GBIF/APHIS/ERA5 data, ecoseek
  stack) vs "built this week" (everything else shown here).
- VO: "Rule check first: the niche-fitting engine, the phylogenies
  and the surveillance data predate this event — every pipeline,
  agent and interface you're about to see was built this week."

## Act 1 — the problem (0:12–0:40)
- Screen: map with HPAI detections layer on. 9,139 wild-bird positives.
- VO: "Highly pathogenic avian influenza moves along flyways. CDC and APHIS see the detections — but for the poultry supply, the question is *where can the next introduction happen?* That's a wildlife susceptibility question, not a surveillance one."

## Act 2 — what we built tonight (0:40–1:30)
- Screen: species picker → suitability layer fades in on a bird species.
- VO: "Tonight, we ran 101,376 ellipsoid niche fits — 88 candidates for each of 1,147 North American species — against 30-year ERA5 climate normals, with each species' accessible area built from its own occupied ecoregions. Every fit got an AIC table, 100% were accepted, and the winner predicts this. Underneath, each species is scored by phylogenetic distance on real trees — BirdTree's posterior for birds, a dated mammal tree — reconciled against GBIF names this week."
- Switch species a couple of times; the map repaints per species.

## Act 3 — the agentic layer (1:30–2:15)
- Screen: decisions delta card, then litmine ticker.
- VO: "An agent watches the campaign — not a cron job. A strict-JSON triage model reads each species' diagnostics, but only the ambiguous tail ever reaches it: near-tied AIC, sparse occurrences, marine ranges. Just 173 fits qualified — and 49 came back `flag_review`, each with a reason. It can drop, flag, or re-fit, and every verdict lands in an auditable delta. The nightly briefing on screen was generated by a second model over tonight's artifacts — baked to JSON, so this demo runs keyless."
- Litmine beat (screen: ticker card): "A second loop reads the
  literature itself: 348 papers → 3,165 full-text passages → a cheap
  triage model keeps the 23% that mention detection → typed
  parasite–host edges at about a tenth of a cent each — and a
  self-hosted judge re-checks every span. Full-text recall beats
  abstracts 3-to-1: 56% vs 16% on the held-out gold."

## Act 4 — the product answer (2:15–3:00)
- Screen: risk layer = detection recency × host-weighted reservoir community, aggregated to HUC8 — named watersheds ranked.
- VO: "Detections alone tell you where the virus *was*. Multiply a recency kernel over those 9,139 detections by the host-weighted reservoir community — each species' suitability times its modelled host probability — and you get risk at 1-kilometre resolution — finer than county — ranked across 2,247 named HUC8 watersheds. At the top tonight: Piscataqua-Salmon Falls, Pamlico Sound, and Cedar-Portage. Actionable by name, not by coordinate. That's WildSeek — live now at wildseek.ecoseek.org, built tonight on Meta's models, served as one process on the EcoSeek stack."
