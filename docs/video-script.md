# WildSeek — 3-minute demo script (draft for polish pass)

## Act 1 — the problem (0:00–0:30)
- Screen: map with HPAI detections layer on. ~9,500 wild-bird positives.
- VO: "Highly pathogenic avian influenza moves along flyways. CDC and
  APHIS see the detections — but the question for poultry supply is
  *where can the next introduction happen*, which is a wildlife
  susceptibility question, not a surveillance one."

## Act 2 — what we built tonight (0:30–1:30)
- Screen: species picker → suit layer fades in on a bird species.
- VO: "We ran 88 ellipsoid niche models per species — 101 thousand fits
  over a thousand North-American vertebrates — against 30-year ERA5
  normals, with each species' accessible area built from its own
  occupied ecoregions. Every one got an AIC table; the winner predicts
  this."
- Switch species a couple of times; the map repaints per species.

## Act 3 — the agentic layer (1:30–2:15)
- Screen: decisions delta or briefing panel.
- VO: "The campaign is watched by an agent, not a cron. A strict-JSON
  triage model reads each species' diagnostics and only the ambiguous
  tail reaches it: near-tied AIC, sparse occurrences, marine ranges.
  It drops, flags, or re-fits — and every verdict lands in an auditable
  delta. The nightly briefing you see is generated by a second model
  over tonight's artifacts."
- Show the delta table: species · verdict · why · AIC old→new.

## Act 4 — the product answer (2:15–3:00)
- Screen: risk layer = detection recency × mean bird suitability,
  aggregated to HUC8 — named watersheds ranked.
- VO: "Detections alone tell you where the virus *was*. Multiply
  recency-weighted surveillance pressure by the community suitability
  surface and you get a 1-kilometre risk map — finer than county —
  ranked to named watersheds so it's actionable. All served as one
  process on the ecoseek stack. Built tonight, on Meta's models."
